# Yuyan Huang (黄于晏)

*Algorithm Engineer · Multimodal Learning & NLP*

I build multimodal AI systems that fuse text, audio, image, and video modalities, with a focus on cross-modal representation learning, sentiment analysis, and LLM-driven applications. My work spans contrastive learning, graph neural networks for aspect-based sentiment classification, and the productionization of multimodal models in industry settings.

Seven years in applied algorithm engineering across multimodal reconstruction, information extraction, and LLM systems — with 40+ granted patents and award-winning shared-task results.

## Research Interests / 研究方向

- **Multimodal Learning:** Cross-modal fusion, reconstruction, and contrastive learning across text, audio, image, and video
- **NLP:** Aspect-based Sentiment Analysis, Information Extraction, Text Classification
- **LLM Systems:** Knowledge injection, retrieval-augmented generation, prompt engineering
